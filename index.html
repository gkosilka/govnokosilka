Support

Frequently asked questions.

How can it be vegan?

Dogs eat grass. The Govnokosilka collects what the dog made of the grass. We simply cut out the middleman. Technically the middleman is the dog. Our lawyers say this counts.4

What does “Govnokosilka” mean?

Говнокосилка, from Russian: govno (we'd rather not say) + kosilka (mower). Our naming agency charged £2.1m and has since gone quiet.

What does it taste like?

It tastes of engineering. Owners describe notes of caramel, freshly cut grass and courage. Flavour varies by breed — see Smoothies.

Does it still mow grass?

Yes, as a secondary function. Clippings are added to each smoothie for fibre and that just-mowed finish.

What about cats?

The Govnokosilka respects a cat's privacy. A dedicated cat module, the KotoKosilka™, is in development for 2028.

I don't have a dog. Can I still use it?

Yes. Our Deposit-as-a-Service subscription delivers a certified Labrador to your lawn twice a week. Lab not included in the box. Lab not returnable.

When will it ship?

Reservations open now; deliveries begin spring 2027. Reserving is free and you can cancel any time — although, medically speaking, why would you?
